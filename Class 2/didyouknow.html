Did you know?

Venus is the only planet in our solar system that spins clockwise. Due to its peculiar rotation, its "day" is actually longer than its "year."

Body Fact

Your lungs have the same surface area as a tennis court. To maximize oxygen absorption, they are filled with tiny air sacs called alveoli

Animal Fact

Cows have best friends and experience severe stress when separated from them.

Geography Fact

Russia has a larger surface area than the entire dwarf planet Pluto.

Technology Fact

The first computer mouse was constructed entirely out of wood in 1964.

Food Fact

Bananas are technically berries, but strawberries and raspberries are not.
