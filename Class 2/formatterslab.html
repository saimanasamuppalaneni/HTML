Pegasus

In Greek mythology, Pegasus is a magnificent, immortal winged stallion born from the blood of the gorgon Medusa after she was beheaded by the hero Perseus. Sired by Poseidon, the god of the sea and horses, this pure-white creature is one of the most recognized figures in ancient lore. Pegasus famously aided the hero Bellerophon in defeating the fire-breathing monster Chimera, but later bucked him off when Bellerophon attempted to fly to Mount Olympus. Following this, the stallion lived among the gods, carrying Zeus's thunderbolts, and was ultimately transformed into a prominent northern constellation.
